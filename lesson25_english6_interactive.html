II. Grammar: Comparative adjectives

1. Short adjectives

Subject + be + adjective-er + than + noun/pronoun
  • My house is smaller than your house.
  • A car is faster than a bike.
  • Lan is taller than Hoa.

2. Long adjectives

Subject + be + more + adjective + than + noun/pronoun
  • This street is more beautiful than that street.
  • English is more interesting than Maths.
  • This exercise is more difficult than that exercise.

VI. Speaking practice

Answer each question in a complete sentence. You can compare your response with the suggested answer.

1. Is the countryside quieter than the city?

Yes, it is. The countryside is quieter than the city.
2. Is the city more crowded than the countryside?

Yes, it is. The city is more crowded than the countryside.
3. Is your house bigger than your friend's house?

No, it isn't. My house is smaller than my friend's house.

VII. Writing

Topic: Compare life in the city and life in the countryside.

Write a paragraph of 50–70 words. Use at least three comparative adjectives.

Word count: 0

Life in the city is different from life in the countryside. The city is more crowded and noisier than the countryside. There are more shops and restaurants in the city. However, the countryside is quieter and more peaceful. Houses in the countryside are sometimes cheaper than houses in the city. I prefer the countryside because I like a quiet life.
