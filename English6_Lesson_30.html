✏️ III. Grammar review

25 minutes · Ôn tập ngữ pháp

1. Present Simple – Thì hiện tại đơn

(+) S + V(s/es).
(−) S + don't/doesn't + V.
(?) Do/Does + S + V?

Examples: I go to school every day. · She doesn't like football. · Does he play tennis?

2. Present Continuous – Thì hiện tại tiếp diễn

S + am/is/are + V-ing.

Examples: I am studying English. · She is watching TV. · They are playing football.

3. Past Simple – Thì quá khứ đơn

S + V2/ed.

Examples: I visited my grandparents yesterday. · She watched TV last night.

4. Future Simple – Thì tương lai đơn

S + will + V nguyên mẫu.

Examples: I will visit Ha Noi tomorrow. · Robots will help people.

5. Comparative adjectives – So sánh hơn

Short adjective + -er + than.
More + long adjective + than.

Examples: My house is bigger than yours. · English is more interesting than Maths.
