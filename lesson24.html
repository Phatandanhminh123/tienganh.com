I. Objectives – Mục tiêu bài học

Sau bài học, học sinh có thể:

  • Nhớ và sử dụng từ vựng về khu phố.
  • Thành lập câu so sánh hơn với tính từ ngắn và tính từ dài.
  • So sánh hai địa điểm bằng câu hoàn chỉnh.
  • Đọc hiểu đoạn văn ngắn và viết đoạn văn khoảng 50–70 từ về khu phố của mình.

III. Grammar – Comparative adjectives

1. So sánh hơn với tính từ ngắn

S + be + adjective-er + than + ...

Ví dụ:

  • My street is cleaner than your street.
    Đường phố của tôi sạch hơn đường phố của bạn.
  • This neighbourhood is quieter than the city centre.
    Khu phố này yên tĩnh hơn trung tâm thành phố.

V. Reading – Đọc hiểu

My Neighbourhood

I live in a small neighbourhood in the countryside. It is quiet and peaceful. There is a school, a market and a beautiful park near my house. The streets are cleaner than those in the city centre. People here are friendly and helpful. The houses are smaller than those in big cities, but life is less stressful. I love my neighbourhood because it is a wonderful place to live.

Questions

1. Where does the writer live?

2. What places are near the writer’s house?

3. Are the streets cleaner than those in the city centre?

4. What are the people like?

5. Why does the writer love the neighbourhood?

1. The writer lives in a small neighbourhood in the countryside.

2. There are a school, a market and a beautiful park near the writer’s house.

3. Yes, they are.

4. They are friendly and helpful.

5. Because it is a wonderful place to live.

VI. Writing – Viết đoạn văn

Topic

Write a paragraph of 50–70 words about your neighbourhood.

Gợi ý

  • Where do you live?
  • Is your neighbourhood quiet or noisy?
  • What places are near your house?
  • What is better about your neighbourhood?
  • Why do you like it?

Useful sentence patterns

  • I live in …
  • My neighbourhood is … and …
  • There is a … near my house.
  • My neighbourhood is quieter/cleaner than …
  • The people are …
  • I like my neighbourhood because …

Your paragraph – Đoạn văn của em

Số từ: 0
Model paragraph:

My neighbourhood is small and peaceful. It is quieter than the city centre. There are some shops, a school and a park near my house. The streets are cleaner than before, and the people are friendlier than those in other places. I love my neighbourhood because it is a comfortable place to live.

VII. Speaking – Luyện nói

Trả lời bằng câu hoàn chỉnh:

  1. Is your neighbourhood quiet or noisy?
  2. Is your neighbourhood cleaner than the city centre?
  3. What is the most convenient place near your house?
  4. Do you like living in your neighbourhood? Why?

Challenge: So sánh khu phố của em với thành phố hoặc vùng nông thôn bằng ít nhất ba câu có than.

1. My neighbourhood is quiet and peaceful.

2. Yes, it is. My neighbourhood is cleaner than the city centre.

3. The market is the most convenient place near my house.

4. Yes, I do, because it is peaceful and the people are friendly.
