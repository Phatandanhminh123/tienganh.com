Lesson 22 – Unit 4: My Neighbourhood

Chủ đề: My Neighbourhood

Trọng tâm: Comparative Adjectives – So sánh hơn

Thời lượng: 90 phút

1. Mục tiêu

Học sinh có thể:

  • Ôn và củng cố cách thành lập tính từ so sánh hơn.
  • Phân biệt -er và more.
  • Sử dụng than chính xác.
  • So sánh các địa điểm trong khu phố.
  • Nói và viết các câu so sánh đơn giản.

3. Grammar – Comparative Adjectives

A. Tính từ ngắn

adjective + -er + than
  • small → smaller
  • tall → taller
  • fast → faster
  • quiet → quieter
My street is quieter than your street.

B. Tính từ tận cùng bằng -y

y → i + er
  • noisy → noisier
  • busy → busier
  • happy → happier
The city is noisier than the countryside.

C. Tính từ tận cùng bằng -e

Chỉ thêm -r
  • large → larger
  • nice → nicer

D. Tính từ có dạng phụ âm – nguyên âm – phụ âm

Gấp đôi phụ âm cuối + -er
  • big → bigger
  • hot → hotter

E. Tính từ dài

more + adjective + than
  • beautiful → more beautiful
  • expensive → more expensive
  • convenient → more convenient
  • modern → more modern
This neighbourhood is more convenient than that one.

F. Tính từ bất quy tắc

Tính từ So sánh hơn
good better
bad worse
This restaurant is better than that restaurant.

5. Practice 2 – Complete the Sentences

Dùng dạng so sánh hơn của từ trong ngoặc.

  1. My house is __________ than yours. (small)
  2. This street is __________ than that street. (busy)
  3. The city centre is __________ than my neighbourhood. (noisy)
  4. This hotel is __________ than that hotel. (expensive)
  5. My neighbourhood is __________ than the city centre. (peaceful)
  6. This park is __________ than that park. (beautiful)
  7. The new shopping centre is __________ than the old one. (modern)
  8. This restaurant is __________ than the restaurant near my house. (good)

6. Practice 3 – Find and Correct the Mistake

1. My house is more small than your house.

→ My house is smaller than your house.

2. This street is noisyer than that street.

→ This street is noisier than that street.

3. The city centre is crowdeder than my neighbourhood.

→ The city centre is more crowded than my neighbourhood.

4. This hotel is expensiver than that hotel.

→ This hotel is more expensive than that hotel.

5. My new school is gooder than my old school.

→ My new school is better than my old school.

7. Speaking – Compare Two Places

Từ gợi ý:

quiet – noisy – crowded – beautiful – expensive – convenient – modern

A: Which is quieter, your street or the city centre?

B: My street is quieter than the city centre.


A: Which is more expensive?

B: The city centre is more expensive.

8. Writing

Viết 6 câu so sánh khu phố của em với một nơi khác.

Gợi ý:

My neighbourhood is quieter than the city centre.

It is less crowded than the city centre.

The city centre is more modern than my neighbourhood.

The city centre is more convenient than my neighbourhood.

My neighbourhood is more peaceful than the city centre.

The city centre is noisier than my neighbourhood.
